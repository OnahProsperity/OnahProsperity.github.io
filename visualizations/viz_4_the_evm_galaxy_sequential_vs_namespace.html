−1 keeps namespaces out of Solidity's path
Sequential slots grow rightward from Slot 0 — Solidity's natural pattern. The −1 in keccak256(keccak256(id) − 1) is a cryptographic pattern-breaker ensuring the outer hash input can never be a valid keccak256 output, scattering myapp.storage and myapp.admin into the opposite region — places Solidity's sequential or hashed slots will never reach.
